Why you can trust Zoe's Colleges

The short version: our numbers come from official federal data, we show our work, and your family's info stays with your family.

Built by a family, for families

Zoe's Colleges started as one parent's own spreadsheet for searching colleges together as a family — and grew into a tool other families could use. It's published by Wotupski Productions. We're a small, independent project, not a big data broker, and not affiliated with any college or ranking company.

We're not a replacement for a school counselor. We're the organized, data-grounded workspace you bring to your counselor.

Where our data comes from

  • Admissions, cost, graduation & earnings — the U.S. Department of Education's College Scorecard, the official federal dataset.
  • Area context (political lean, gun & LGBTQ policy) — state-level tallies from Cook PVI / presidential results, the Giffords Law Center, and the Movement Advancement Project. These describe the state/area, not the school.
  • Deadlines & extra detail — curated by us; always confirm on the school's own site.

Every school's detail page names its sources, and nothing here is a number we made up.

How our projections work — and their limits

Match % and admission chance (Reach / Target / Likely) are projections, not promises. They combine your grades, course rigor, test scores and activities with each school's published data, using logic drawn from widely-respected sources (NACAC's admission-factors survey, the standard GPA recalculation, the common 4-tier activities scale).

We show the weighting in the open and let you adjust it. No model can see essays, interviews, or the hundreds of human judgments real admissions involve — so treat every result as guidance on a spectrum, not certainty, and review it with a counselor.

Your privacy & your data

  • Your profile, ratings, school list, notes and photos are visible only to your own family — enforced at the database level, not just hidden in the app.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't share it with colleges or advertisers.
  • You can remove schools, photos and your account's data at any time.
  • It's an invite-only beta, free while we build it. For ages 13 and up.

No online service can promise perfect security, but we keep your data minimal and family-scoped by design. Questions or a privacy request? Contact us.

How we calculate this

Everything here is a projection, not a promise — built from published admissions logic and shown in the open. Treat it as guidance on a spectrum and review it with a counselor. You can adjust the weights yourself in My Profile.

Match % (fit for you)

A weighted blend of what you tell us matters, scored against each school's data:

  • Value, Education, Selectivity, Spirit, Proximity, Lifestyle — your importance sliders set how much each counts.
  • Size & setting, political lean, gun & LGBTQ policy, religion, nearby — matched to your stated preferences.
  • Move any slider and every school re-scores instantly. Nothing is fixed.

Admission chance (Reach / Target / Likely)

  • Starts from the school's admit rate (more selective → harder band).
  • Adjusted by your test scores vs. the school's middle-50% range (25th–75th percentile) where we have it — scoring above the 75th helps, below the 25th tightens the band; we fall back to the average otherwise (ACT converted to SAT via the official concordance).
  • Your GPA is rigor-adjusted — Honors +0.5, AP/IB +1.0, dual-enrollment +0.5 (the standard colleges use).
  • Your activities (4-tier scale) can nudge a Reach toward Target at selective schools — but they're capped one step and never override your grades.
  • For most selective schools we have the 25th–75th percentile score range (federal IPEDS data); for the rest, just the average. Either way the read is a band, not a false percentage.

Your high school (context)

  • Colleges read your transcript against your own school's profile — the counselor sends a School Profile listing what courses your school offers. Taking most of the APs available reads stronger than a few out of many, and a school with few APs is never held against you.
  • Tell us your school's AP/Honors offerings in your profile and we judge your rigor in that context (it can ease a reach at selective schools, capped one step).
  • What we don't do: we do not claim a specific college "prefers" a certain district or high school. No college publishes that data, so inventing a number would break our honesty rule. The real per-school signal is your Naviance/Scoir scattergram — which is your school's private data to bring, not something any app can pull.

List balance

A balanced list has a mix: roughly 2–4 Likely, 3–5 Target, 2–6 Reach. We grade your board against that so you can see gaps at a glance.
