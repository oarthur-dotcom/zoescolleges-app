How Zoe's Colleges works
  1. Add schools — anyone in the family can add or remove them.
  2. Rate with stars — tap your row of stars on each school. You only control your own stars; you can see everyone's.
  3. Invite your family — share your code so the rest of your family can rate too.
  4. Watch the family average — schools sort by it, so your shared favorites rise to the top.
