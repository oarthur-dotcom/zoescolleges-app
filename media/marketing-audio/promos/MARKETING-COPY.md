## Our story (short — bios, About blurb, 1-liner ads)
We're a family who went through the college search. It got stressful fast: too many tabs, too many deadlines, too many opinions. So we built the thing we wished we'd had: one shared place where everyone plans together. The hard parts get handled, and the good parts are what you remember.

## Our story (long — About page, landing page)
When Zoe started looking at colleges, our house turned into a command center. Spreadsheets. Brochures. A group text nobody could find anything in. Every conversation about the future started to feel like a status meeting.

That's not how it should feel. This is one of the last big things a family does together before a kid leaves home. It should be exciting. It should be memorable.

So we built Zoe's Colleges. It tracks the lists, the deadlines, the checklists, the costs and the chances, so you don't have to. That leaves you free for the stuff that matters: the classes that light your kid up, the teams and clubs, the campus that just feels right, and the road trips you'll talk about for years.

Students, parents, even grandparents all see the same plan. Nobody's left out, and nobody has to be the family's project manager.

Let us handle the details. You enjoy the ride.
